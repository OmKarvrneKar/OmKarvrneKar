## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 💰 [finance_agent](https://github.com/OmKarvrneKar) · AI Personal Finance Platform
> Upload bank statements and receipts, then ask questions about your spending in plain English, with answers grounded in your real data.

- Ingests **bank CSV statements and receipt images** (Pandas + Tesseract OCR); **Gemini** categorizes transactions and powers a natural-language finance agent.
- **Deterministic finance engine kept separate from the LLM:** budgets, moving-average forecasting, anomaly detection, subscription tracking, and merchant analytics are computed in code, so the AI **cannot fabricate financial figures**.
- **Secure multi-user auth:** JWT with server-side token revocation, HttpOnly cookies, rate limiting, and user-scoped queries.
- Validated with **500+ automated backend and frontend tests**. Deployed on Vercel + Render.

`React` `FastAPI` `Google Gemini` `Pandas` `Tesseract OCR` `JWT`

---

### 🧾 [Smart_Invoice](https://github.com/OmKarvrneKar) · Invoice Fraud Detection Platform
> Explainable, auditable invoice review with fraud risk scoring.

- **87% field-extraction accuracy** (vendor, date, amount) across 20–35 test invoices using confidence-weighted OCR parsing.
- **Rule-based + anomaly-driven fraud risk scoring** with duplicate detection and per-field confidence metrics.
- Secure **human-in-the-loop review system**: JWT authentication, full audit logging, REST APIs, PostgreSQL.

`FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `Tesseract OCR` `JWT`

---

### 🧭 [Open Source Companion](https://github.com/OmKarvrneKar) · ML-Powered OSS Issue Recommender
> Helps developers find and contribute to the right open-source GitHub issues.

- Three custom ML models: **TF-IDF + XGBoost** issue-difficulty classifier, **Sentence-BERT + FAISS** semantic recommender, and a **Random Forest** PR-success predictor.
- Async backend (**Celery + Redis + PostgreSQL**) that listens to **GitHub webhooks**, detects merged contributions, and drives a gamification system (points, badges, leaderboard).
- Clean service contract `get_recommendations(user_profile, n=10)` returning ranked `issue_id / match_score` pairs, decoupling the ML layer from the app backend.

`Python` `XGBoost` `Sentence-BERT` `FAISS` `Celery` `Redis` `PostgreSQL`

---

### 🩺 [Medical Report Explainer](https://github.com/OmKarvrneKar) · Healthcare AI
> Turns complex lab reports into plain-language explanations in 6 languages.

- OCR extracts text from **PDF/image reports**; **Gemini** translates lab results into simple, understandable explanations.
- **Multilingual output** in English, Hindi, Marathi, Kannada, Tamil, and Telugu, with text-to-speech readout and historical report tracking (SQLite).
- Built and deployed on Vercel + Render; output validated across multiple test reports.

`React` `FastAPI` `Gemini` `Tesseract` `PyMuPDF` `SQLite`
