I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at **MVJ College of Engineering, Bangalore**, specializing in **AI/ML** and **full-stack development**, and most recently an **AI/ML Engineer Intern** at Aigetai. I independently design, build, and deploy complete AI-powered applications — from LLM-driven personal finance agents and OCR-based invoice fraud detection to ML recommendation engines and multilingual medical report translation — taking each from architecture through to live deployment.

My engineering approach centers on **explainability, security, and real-world reliability**: keeping deterministic logic separate from LLM output so the AI can't fabricate numbers, confidence-weighted extraction pipelines, JWT-secured human-in-the-loop review systems, and well-tested, audit-logged APIs rather than black-box demos.
